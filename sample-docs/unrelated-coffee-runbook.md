# Third Floor Kitchen: Coffee Machine Runbook

**Maintained by:** Helena Duarte, Workplace
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-02
**Applies to:** the espresso machine on the third floor, and the grinder next to it

## What this machine is

A two-group commercial espresso machine. It has a steam wand, a grinder, and a
drip tray that needs emptying more often than anyone admits.

The grinder burrs were replaced in March. The steam wand gasket was replaced in June
after it started weeping. Everything else is original to the machine.

## Why we do not have a milk frother

The budget for a proper automatic frother was declined twice. The manual wand does the
job. You have to learn the technique. It is not complicated, but people who have not
been told will absolutely overheat the milk and then blame the machine.

If the milk looks like soup, it is too hot. Aim for something that feels like a warm
shower, not a bath.

## Daily, before 9am

1. Turn the machine on. It takes about four minutes to reach temperature.
2. Run water through both groups for fifteen seconds.
3. Run the grinder empty for ten seconds to clear old grounds.
4. Empty the drip tray.

## Weekly

- Backflush the groups. Tablet, no detergent.
- Wipe the steam wand immediately after every use. Do not let it dry. This is the
  single most important thing on this page and the thing everyone skips.

## Known problems

The grinder makes a noise that sounds alarming. It is not alarming. The sound is the
burrs settling and it has done it since the machine was installed.

If the machine stops heating, check the breaker under the counter first. It has tripped
maybe four times this year and every time it was somebody's kettle on the same circuit.

The drip tray overflow indicator has never once been correct.
